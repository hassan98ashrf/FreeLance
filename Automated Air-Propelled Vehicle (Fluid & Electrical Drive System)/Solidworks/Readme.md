# 📐 CAD Models (SolidWorks)

## 📌 Overview
This directory contains the complete mechanical design of the vehicle, modeled in SolidWorks. The designs transition the conceptual aerodynamic requirements into a manufacturable prototype.

## 🛠️ Included Designs
 3D Parts & Assemblies Fully constrained 3D models of the chassis base, 3-blade propeller (45° pitch), motor mount, and sensor brackets.
 Electronic Packaging Modeled placements for the Arduino Nano, L298N Motor Driver, and breadboard to ensure optimal weight distribution and wire routing.
 2D Engineering Drawings Standard ANSI detailed 2D drawings with precise dimensions and tolerances, ready for manufacturing or laser cutting.
