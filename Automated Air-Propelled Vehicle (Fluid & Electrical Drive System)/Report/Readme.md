# 📄 Final Engineering Report

## 📌 Overview
This folder contains the comprehensive Final Engineering Report for the Automated Air-Propelled Vehicle project. 

## 🔍 Report Highlights
* **Fluid & Mechanical Systems:** In-depth mathematical modeling of the propeller thrust, aerodynamic efficiency, and dynamic scaling.
* **Electrical Systems:** Power consumption analysis, motor efficiency calculations, and L298N driver power loss evaluations.
* **Risk Assessment:** FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) covering structural integrity, sensor reliability, and power management.
* **Sustainability & Economics:** A professional Bill of Quantity (BoQ), material lifecycle analysis (using recycled components), and the project's overall environmental impact.
